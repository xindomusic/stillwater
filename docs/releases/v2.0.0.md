# Stillwater 2.0 — Go fishing

Released 18 October 2026 · Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

Stillwater 2.0 hands you a rod. Open a fishing window onto your own aquarium, choose a hook and a bait or lure, cast, and watch the float. Your fish look it over, pick at it, steal it, or take it, and if you strike at the right moment you play the fish in, admire it, and let it go. Your desktop aquarium carries on undisturbed.

## What's new

### Go fishing
- Open it from the fish menu (**Go Fishing…**), with **⌘G** in Stillwater, or from **Settings → Fishing**.
- **Click the water** to cast there. Under a float, the bait hangs at the depth you click.
- **Click** (or Space) to strike, or to twitch the bait. **Hold** to reel in or retrieve a lure. **Esc** brings the line straight in.
- With a bare hook, one click on the water rebaits and casts again.

### Tackle that matters
| Hook | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| No. 18 fine wire | Small mouths: rasboras, barbs, danios, loaches, bettas. Light: play a big fish gently or it opens out. |
| No. 12 J-hook | Mid-sized mouths like the pearl gourami. Strike when the float slides under. |
| No. 6 circle hook | The koi. It sets itself as the fish turns, so don't strike. Small fish just strip it. |

Baits hang under a float: **bloodworm** (everyone's favourite, soft), **bread** (koi and gouramis; it crumbles away), and a **soaked pellet**. Lures go without one: a **dry fly** on the surface film, a **micro spinner** that only flashes while you reel, and a **soft worm jig** hopped along the sand.

### Fish that behave like fish
- They find bait by scent and lures by movement. Each one swims over, looks it over, and then refuses it, nibbles bits off the hook, or takes it and turns away as the float slides under.
- Small fish strip a big hook bare. Shrimp and crabs steal bait off the sand, and a crab may cling on as you reel it up.
- Splashes and fights scatter shy fish, while bold ones come to see whether it's feeding time.
- A fish that feels the hook stays wary for minutes, and a little warier for good after each catch.

### A fight with real risk
- Small fish are swung straight out.
- Barbs and gouramis dive and shake their heads. Horse one and the hook tears free.
- A koi runs and takes line through the drag. Reel while it rests and let go when it runs, or the line snaps. Let the line go slack and it throws the hook.

### Readable at a glance
- The float lies flat until the split shot cocks it upright. If it stays flat, a fish took the bait on the drop.
- Pecks ripple the film, an orange pulse marks the moment to strike, and at night the float has a glowing tip.
- A tension meter with a RUN badge, a bait meter, and plain messages tell you what is happening.
- Soft optional sounds and VoiceOver announcements cover the key moments.

### Catch and release
- Every fish is measured (each one keeps its own size) and weighed, then shown on a catch card that flags personal bests and new species. Then it is released.
- **Settings → Fishing** keeps a lifetime catch log of all eight hookable species, with field notes on what each one takes, plus a tackle guide.

## Install or upgrade

1. Quit Stillwater if it is running (menu bar icon → **Quit**).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Stillwater** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy.
3. Open Stillwater. Your settings and population are kept.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust this release, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. The [installation guide](../INSTALL.md) has step-by-step instructions.

## Known limitations
- Fishing happens in its own window. The desktop aquarium stays click-through and never has a line in it.
- Fish sizes on the catch card follow the real species, while the drawn fish are tuned for the wallpaper. The tank is a decorative world, not a stocking guide.
- A fish turning with the bait in its mouth briefly shows the line meeting it mid-body.
- Older notes from 1.8.0 still apply: saved wallpaper stills build up until you remove them, and shrimp only half hide behind the two smallest rocks in Sunken Grove.

## Under the hood
- The fishing model (`Fishing.swift`, `SimulationFishing.swift`) is deterministic and replayable. With no line in the water, every fish makes exactly the same choices as in 1.8.
- New model checks cover casting physics, bait theft, strike timing, self-setting circle hooks, fight technique, catch and release, lures, bed thieves, Still freezing, non-interference, and the catch log. A new rendering check verifies the drawn rig.
- `tools/fishing-transcript.sh` lets scripted anglers fish every hook and bait headlessly, and CI runs it. `tools/fishing-capture.sh` renders fishing moments and the window for review.
