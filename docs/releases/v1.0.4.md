# Stillwater 1.0.4

Each fish now chooses its own route.

- **Mixed directions:** mostly small course changes, some wider turns, and occasional reversals. No shared looping path or prescribed full-circle turn.
- **Individual pace:** fish vary their speed, turn rate, and time between decisions. Some keep swimming while others rest or make a short dash.
- **Looser gathering:** schooling fish respond gently to nearby companions, with changing interest in staying together or exploring.
- **Independent randomness:** separate streams for each fish's navigation, moods, feeding responses, and fin strokes; individual bubble respawns and pellet variations.
- Articulated bodies, fin motion, particle depth, clear water, day/night settings, and Still-mode freezing remain in place.

See [validation](https://github.com/xindomusic/stillwater/blob/main/VALIDATION.md) for tests and performance measurements. The renderer remains a lightweight photographic approximation of fish motion.

Quit the previous app before installing the DMG or ZIP. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14+. The app is ad-hoc signed, **not Apple-notarized**. [Installation instructions](https://github.com/xindomusic/stillwater/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).
