# Stillwater 1.5.0 — Crabs whose legs keep up, loaches that dart and rest

Released 24 September 2026 · Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

This release reworks how the bottom of the tank moves. A crab's legs now step exactly as far as its body travels, and loaches dart, coast, turn, and rest like real kuhli loaches instead of gliding at one speed. Bubbles are calm again, and the sand bed has depth.

## What's new

### Crab legs that match the walk
- The step cycle now advances with the **distance the crab actually travels**, so a planted foot stays put on the sand while the body moves over it. This holds when the crab walks sideways, forward or back, or gets nudged by a neighbour.
- When a crab stops, each lifted foot **comes down where it is** instead of sliding back to a rest pose.
- The shell **bobs with each step** while the feet stay planted, and the walking pace surges slightly with every stride.
- The rear legs can now reach their full stride.

### Calmer, more purposeful crabs
- Crabs walk in **bouts of a few steps**, mostly carrying on the same way instead of pacing back and forth.
- When something is in the way, a crab usually **waits or sidesteps** up or down the sand, and only sometimes turns back.
- A crab is no longer shoved along by passing loaches. Crabs crowding the same pellet **wait their turn** behind the one nearest it.
- Crabs leave the refuge edges to the shrimp that hide there.

### Loaches that move like loaches
- Loaches travel in **quick wriggling darts** of one to three body lengths, strongest at the start, then **pause to sift**. Each dart veers a little, so paths curve across the sand.
- The body wave follows the distance travelled and beats **two to three times a second** in a dart. It fades as the loach coasts to a stop, so the body straightens at rest.
- A loach **swims round in a curve** to turn back instead of spinning in place, and veers around neighbours and crabs ahead.
- At rest, each loach lies with **its own slight curve and angle**. It lowers its head gently and pecks at the sand in irregular spells, and its tail ripples slowly.
- Loaches keep a little more room from each other, so two rarely read as one long fish.

### Calm bubbles and a deeper sand bed
- **No more streams**: single bubbles now and then drift slowly up from the sand or a plant leaf, swaying gently, and take several seconds to reach the surface.
- The sand bed has **perspective**. Bottom dwellers lower on the bed are nearer the glass and larger; those farther back sit higher and smaller. Turning on the sand carries them up or down the bed.

### Fixes
- Hidden shrimp are no longer pushed out of their refuge by crabs.

## Install or upgrade

1. Quit Stillwater if it is running (menu bar icon → **Quit**).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Stillwater** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy.
3. Open Stillwater. Your settings and population are kept.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust this release, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. The [installation guide](../INSTALL.md) has step-by-step instructions.

## Known limitations
- Loaches sometimes still swim close side by side or pass over one another, and a loach can pass in front of or behind a crab so that it appears to touch the crab's legs.
- When a crab walks straight toward or away from you, its legs sweep up and down the screen and can look a little stilted.
- Seen exactly head-on, a shrimp or loach briefly shows as a small rounded shape, because the artwork is a side-on photograph.

## Under the hood
- New `stepCrabLegs` in the simulation drives the crab's gait from its actual displacement. The crab shader takes the walking axis, how settled the legs are, and the shell bob through `a_curve`.
- New `BurstRhythm` gives each loach its dart-and-pause rhythm, with a per-dart bearing and resting pose. The loach's head dip and resting ripple run on their own clock.
- A new test covers loach darts and pauses, and the body wave following travel. The crab foot rendering check follows the new rear-leg stance. The model and rendering tests all pass. Full history: [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
