# Stillwater 1.7.0 — Loaches that rest and forage, shrimp that pick and hide, bubbles like glass beads

Released 25 September 2026 · Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

This release reworks the three things that live closest to the sand. Each was studied against how the real animal (or bubble) behaves, then refined over repeated side-by-side reviews.

## What's new

### Loaches with a kuhli's rhythm
- Loaches now **rest for a quarter to a full minute** at a time: sometimes at the foot of a rock, sometimes lying alongside another loach, each with its own curve and angle.
- Between rests they **forage slowly with the nose down in the sand**, slithering on in long, unhurried stretches, and now and then **roam** more briskly.
- Speeds are **mixed**: mostly still or slow, some steady swimming, and occasional **quick darts**, sometimes a **zigzag burst of play** that a neighbour joins.
- The head stays down while foraging instead of flicking up, and a loach setting off often gets a paused neighbour moving too.
- A loach never darts into the end of the bed or into a neighbour, and one lying at rest doesn't turn round where it lies.

### Shrimp that graze and hide like cherry shrimp
- Standing to graze, the **front claws pick at the sand** in quick strokes, each shrimp to its own rhythm.
- The legs **step exactly as far as the shrimp walks**; the antennae wave, and the swimmerets fan now and then at rest.
- A shrimp **hides by walking behind a real rock** in the scene and comes back out past its edge.
- A tail flip always drives it **tail first**, the abdomen **curling under the body**, and it settles back onto the sand afterwards.
- Shrimp no longer sparkle at desktop size, stand on loaches' backs, or vanish on open sand.

### Bubbles like beads of glass
- Bubbles are **small and slow**, rising about 2–3 cm/s: a dark lower edge, a brighter middle, a silver rim and a glint. The tiniest show as a speck with a point of light.
- **Leaves grow a bead** for several seconds, which draws up and lets go.
- At the surface a bubble **slows, meets its own mirrored reflection**, rests a moment and winks out.
- There are **far fewer** of them, released one at a time from spots on the sand and on real leaves in each scene.

## Install or upgrade

1. Quit Stillwater if it is running (menu bar icon → **Quit**).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Stillwater** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy.
3. Open Stillwater. Your settings and population are kept.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust this release, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. The [installation guide](../INSTALL.md) has step-by-step instructions.

## Known limitations
- Behind the two small rocks in Sunken Grove, which are barely wider than a shrimp, a hiding shrimp only half disappears.
- A shrimp still turns round mostly on the spot, briefly showing a head-on view, and can spend a while walking to cover.
- After a tail flip a shrimp can occasionally land on driftwood before settling back to the sand.
- Loaches rest beside a rock more often than beside each other.

## Under the hood
- `BurstRhythm` gives each loach rest, forage and roam regimes, rest spots, bursts of play and group responses; bed lanes treat the end of the bed as an obstacle.
- Shrimp refuges are domes fitted to rocks in each scene's artwork and passed to the shader as `a_rock`; claws, legs and the tail curl are driven in the shrimp shader.
- `BubbleField` now draws beads on leaves, rising bubbles and surfaced bubbles with their reflections, and the bubble shader has bead, tiny and reflection styles.
- All model tests and rendering checks pass. Full history: [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
