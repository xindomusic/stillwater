# Stillwater 1.6.0 — Loaches that keep their lanes, crabs that look where they walk

Released 24 September 2026 · Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

This release is about how the animals on the sand share it. Loaches no longer swim through one another or through crabs, crabs no longer walk over their neighbours, and a loach that is hemmed in works its way free instead of parking.

## What's new

### Loaches keep their lanes
- A loach **never darts into a neighbour or a crab**. Setting off with someone close ahead, it turns back if there is room behind, or **crawls diagonally across to another lane**, body angled toward the new spot.
- A neighbour moving into its lane **ends the dart**, and the loach coasts to a stop short of them.
- Loaches no longer pass through one another, stack, or line up nose to tail. One boxed in by neighbours squeezes through a narrower gap after a few seconds and, if still stuck, turns back and swims round.
- A loach that has just turned around does not turn straight back again.
- Darts are **strongest at the start and ease off**, topping out at about two body lengths a second.

### Loaches and crabs
- A loach and a crab keep a **box of room** between them, so a loach no longer lies with its snout across a crab's legs, and a darting loach gives crabs a wide berth.
- A crab is never shoved along by a loach.

### Crabs look where they walk
- A crab walking **toward or away from you** now stops or sidesteps for whoever lies across its path, instead of walking over a resting crab or loach.
- Its stride in that direction is drawn **foreshortened**, so the legs no longer look like stilts, while the feet still plant.

## Install or upgrade

1. Quit Stillwater if it is running (menu bar icon → **Quit**).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Stillwater** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy.
3. Open Stillwater. Your settings and population are kept.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust this release, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. The [installation guide](../INSTALL.md) has step-by-step instructions.

## Known limitations
- On a bed crowded with several crabs and loaches, a loach can wait some seconds for a gap before it moves on.
- Two loaches can still briefly overlap as one settles beside another.
- Seen exactly head-on, a shrimp or loach briefly shows as a small rounded shape, because the artwork is a side-on photograph.

## Under the hood
- `keepDartLaneClear`, `bedFreeDistance`, `bedIsClearAcross`, and `bedSeparation` in the simulation give bottom dwellers a shared sense of lanes on the bed. `BurstRhythm` gained sidesteps and a parked-time escape.
- The crab shader draws vertical strides at half length (`crabVerticalStride`), with the gait clock advancing to match so planted feet never slide.
- All model tests and rendering checks pass. Full history: [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
