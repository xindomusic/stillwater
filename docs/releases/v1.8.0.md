# Stillwater 1.8.0 — Saved wallpapers you can see and manage, faithfully lit exports

Released 17 October 2026 · Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

This release is about the moments you keep. Stills set as a macOS wallpaper are no longer ever deleted behind your back, exports capture the light exactly as you see it, and Settings shows what your saved stills are holding. Underneath, the codebase moved to Swift 6 and a tidier simulation layout, with no intended change to how anything swims.

## What's new

### Your saved wallpapers, kept — and accounted for
- Stills saved as a macOS wallpaper are **retained until you remove them**. macOS only reports the wallpaper of connected displays on the current Space, so an older still that looks unused may still be showing somewhere; Stillwater no longer guesses.
- **Motion & light** settings now show **how many stills are saved and how much space they take**, with an **Open folder** button, so cleanup is a deliberate choice rather than a surprise in either direction.

### Exports keep the light you see
- Saving a PNG or setting a still wallpaper **during a day/night fade** now preserves the lighting at that exact moment, instead of snapping to full day or full night.

## Install or upgrade

1. Quit Stillwater if it is running (menu bar icon → **Quit**).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Stillwater** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy.
3. Open Stillwater. Your settings and population are kept.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust this release, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. The [installation guide](../INSTALL.md) has step-by-step instructions.

## Known limitations
- Saved wallpaper stills accumulate until you remove them from their folder; the new Settings summary keeps that visible.
- Behind the two small rocks in Sunken Grove, which are barely wider than a shrimp, a hiding shrimp only half disappears.
- A shrimp still turns round mostly on the spot, briefly showing a head-on view, and can spend a while walking to cover.

## Under the hood
- Every target now compiles in **Swift 6 language mode** with strict concurrency checking; main-thread texture caches are explicitly marked rather than assumed.
- Species-only behavior moved into a **`SpeciesBehavior` enum payload** on each fish, and the simulation split into `Simulation.swift`, `SimulationFeeding.swift`, and `SimulationBed.swift`.
- Crab gait constants are **generated into the shader** from the simulation's tuning values, with a rendering test asserting the generated literals.
- Build and test scripts share **one source list** (`tools/sources.zsh`); the rendering tests explain the graphical-session requirement instead of dying silently, and the performance probe reports **budgets** for frame rate, memory footprint, and Still-mode CPU.
- All 36 model tests, all rendering checks, and the native `--review` verification pass. Full history: [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
