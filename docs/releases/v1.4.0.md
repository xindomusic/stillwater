# Stillwater 1.4.0 — Crabs that walk, and life on the sand

Released 23 September 2026 · Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

This release rebuilds how the bottom of the tank moves. Crabs walk on real jointed legs, shrimp step and swim with their legs and swimmerets, loaches work the sand, and the sand itself responds.

## What's new

### Crabs that walk like crabs
- The eight walking legs are now **jointed limbs** rather than a stretched photograph, so legs no longer tear or leave floating pieces behind.
- Each leg has a **raised knee**, and its **foot stays planted on the sand** while the body moves over it, then lifts and swings forward to the next foothold.
- Legs step in the **alternating diagonal gait** of real crabs, with four feet down at any moment; faster walking takes longer strides, and the body bobs slightly with each step.
- The shell, eyes, and claws stay photographic, and the legs are coloured from the photographed legs, with darker joints and tapered tips. A resting crab picks at the sand with its small claws.
- Crabs hold a **compact, low stance**, and keep clear of loaches, shrimp, and each other instead of climbing over them.

### Shrimp
- Walking legs **step in a wave** running from the back legs forward, and each leg swings as a whole limb.
- **Swimmerets beat** under the tail while swimming, and legs tuck in whenever a shrimp is off the sand.
- Grazing shrimp stay on the sand, and shrimp fleeing the same fish pick their own hiding spots instead of stacking up.

### Loaches on the sand
- Loaches **ripple along their whole length** like eels as they work over the bed.
- When sifting, a loach **tips only its head down into the sand**; its body and tail stay on the bottom.
- Every bottom dweller rests on a **soft contact shadow**, which shortens when a fish faces you.

### Living sand
- Loaches sifting, crabs setting off, shrimp picking, and fish taking food off the bottom **throw up fine grains in the sand's own colour** that arc up and settle back.
- A soft **silt cloud** lingers behind the animal that raised it.
- The sand beds in Sunken Grove and Willow Springs are wider, giving bottom dwellers room to spread out.

### Fixes
- A loach's head no longer tears while it sifts.
- Shrimp and crab legs no longer leave detached fragments.
- A rendering check no longer depends on the display's pixel density.

## Install or upgrade

1. Quit Stillwater if it is running (menu bar icon → **Quit**).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Stillwater** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy.
3. Open Stillwater. Your settings and population are kept.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust this release, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. The [installation guide](../INSTALL.md) has step-by-step instructions.

## Known limitations
- Seen exactly head-on, a shrimp or loach briefly shows as a small rounded shape, because the artwork is a side-on photograph.
- A fleeing shrimp can hover in open water for a second before it settles.
- Occasionally a shrimp walks across a resting loach, or crabs bunch up at food.

## Under the hood
- The crab shader draws legs with two-bone inverse kinematics and a per-pixel leg map built from the photograph; a new `a_pixel` attribute gives drawn edges one screen pixel of antialiasing.
- New and updated tests cover the crab's planted feet, crab-on-loach overlap, bed spacing for every pair of species, sand settling, and hover and turn behaviour. Full history: [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
