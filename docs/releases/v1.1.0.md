# Stillwater 1.1.0

A larger community, with steadier swimming.

- **Natural posture:** fish keep a modest tilt, ease back toward level during turns and rests, and turn their heads before travelling in the opposite direction. Independent routes, variable pace, and pauses remain.
- **Six new residents:** celestial pearl danios, golden barbs, blue bettas, Kohaku koi, cherry shrimp, and Thai micro crabs join the original four species. Photographic-style artwork brings tiny, medium, and large animals in blue, gold, red, ivory, and silver tones.
- **Residents controls:** browse small fish, larger fish, or shrimp and crabs. Set each count separately, or try the Colorful mix and Bottom garden presets.
- **Substrate behavior:** shrimp graze, drift in different directions, seek cover, hide, and emerge again. Crabs crawl in varied directions using eight individually phased walking legs, with small body turns. Shells stay rigid, and both animals react to sinking food.
- **Bounded workload:** new textures load on demand into compact buffers. The community remains capped at 120 residents, with up to 30 of one species.

Your existing settings and population are preserved. New species start at zero; add them through **Residents** in settings.

Model tests cover all ten species, upright posture, feeding, persistence, population limits, and Still mode. Native rendering checks cover continuous turns, independent appendage motion, rigid shells, and the existing ghosting regressions. Production app checks and CPU/memory samples are documented in [validation](../../VALIDATION.md).

Download the DMG or ZIP and replace your existing app after quitting it. Requires **Apple Silicon and macOS 14+**. The binary is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**; first-launch instructions are in the [installation guide](../INSTALL.md). SHA-256 checksums accompany the release.

Source, documentation, and bundled artwork remain public under the MIT license.
