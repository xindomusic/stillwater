# Stillwater 1.3.0

Real bubbles, real food, and fish that turn like fish.

- **Bubbles you can see:** streams rise from small porous air stones bedded in the sand: a dense curtain of millimetre bubbles mixed with a fine mist, a fainter stream farther back, and tiny bubbles from plant leaves. They rise at measured speeds; larger bubbles flatten, rock, and zigzag, with a clear centre and a silvery rim, and distant ones soften.
- **Realistic food:** small, round, soaked micro-pellets with a wet sheen and their own tints, sinking nearly straight. Farther pellets are smaller, bluer, and out of focus, and settled ones rest on the sand.
- **Fish turn like fish:** the body curves into the turn head first, then the tail sweeps back past straight and settles, as measured in koi. Small fish flick round quickly; koi, gouramis, loaches, and bettas swing through wide, unhurried arcs. Hovering gouramis and bettas turn on their fins with a straight body, and their long fins stay visible when they face you.
- **Crabs and shrimp:** crabs scuttle sideways in short bursts, stop when they bump a neighbour, and keep off the loaches. Shrimp pick their way across the sand and escape fish that would eat them in bursts of tail flips, backward and upward with the abdomen curled, then sink back down.
- **Living sand:** loaches sifting, crabs setting off, shrimp picking, and fish taking food off the bottom kick up grains in the sand's own colour, with a pale silt cloud. Bottom dwellers lie side by side rather than piling up.
- **Fixes:** fish no longer linger for minutes after a meal before they can feed again.

Your settings and population are preserved.

Motion values follow published measurements of koi turns, shrimp tail flips, crab walking, air-bubble rise, and fish-food sinking; see the [development notes](../DEVELOPMENT.md) and [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md). Model tests now cover two-stage turns, hover turns, bed spacing, sand, bubble and pellet physics, and shrimp and crab gaits.

Requires **Apple Silicon and macOS 14+**. The binary is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**; first-launch instructions are in the [installation guide](../INSTALL.md).
