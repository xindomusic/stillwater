# Stillwater 1.3.0 — Real bubbles, real food, and fish that turn like fish

Released 23 September 2026 · Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later

This release makes the aquarium behave like a real tank. Bubbles, food, turning fish, crabs, shrimp, and the sand were all reworked from published measurements of how these animals and materials actually move, then checked over repeated visual reviews.

## What's new

### Bubbles you can actually see
- Streams now rise from small porous **air stones bedded in the sand**, with sand in the scene's own colour heaped against them.
- A dense curtain of millimetre bubbles leaves pores all along each stone, mixed with a **fine mist**; a second stream rises farther back, and plant leaves release the odd tiny bubble.
- Bubbles rise at **measured speeds** (about 12 cm/s at 1 mm, levelling off near 22 cm/s). Tiny ones stay round and rise straight; larger ones flatten, rock, and zigzag.
- Each bubble shows a **clear centre with a small upside-down view** of the water behind, a thin dark band, and a **silvery rim**; distant bubbles are smaller and softer.
- If you had bubbles turned off, turn them on under **Motion & light → Bubbles**.

### Realistic food
- Food is now small, round, **soaked micro-pellets** (about 0.8–1.6 mm) with a wet sheen and their own tan-to-red-brown tints.
- Pellets sink slowly and **nearly straight**, and settle on the sand.
- Farther pellets are **smaller, bluer, and softly out of focus**, so a sprinkle has real depth.

### Fish turn like fish, not like a toy
- A turn now has **two stages**, as measured in koi: the body curves into the turn **head first**, then the **tail sweeps back** past straight and settles.
- **Small fish** (rasboras, cherry barbs, danios, golden barbs) flick round quickly with a deep bend.
- **Koi, gouramis, loaches, and bettas** swing through wider, unhurried arcs instead of spinning on the spot, and never sprint to make a turn.
- **Hovering gouramis and bettas** turn gently on their fins with a straight body.
- A curled tail stays visible when a fish faces you mid-turn, and **betta and gourami fins fan out** instead of collapsing into a line.
- Koi and gouramis cruise a little faster, closer to real fish.

### Crabs and shrimp
- **Crabs** scuttle sideways in short bursts with pauses, stop and turn back when they bump a neighbour, and no longer climb over loaches.
- **Shrimp** pick their way across the sand in small steps. Fish that would eat them (barbs, gouramis, bettas, koi) set off an escape: a burst of one to three **tail flips backward and upward, with the abdomen curled**, then the shrimp sinks back down. Loaches and danios leave them in peace.

### Living sand
- Loaches pausing to sift, crabs setting off, shrimp picking, and fish taking food off the bottom **kick up sand** in the scene's own colour, with a pale silt cloud that settles.
- Bottom dwellers **lie side by side** instead of piling up, and the bed recedes naturally: animals farther back sit a little higher.

### Fixes
- Fish no longer linger for minutes after a meal before they can feed again.
- The settings label **Tiny bubbles** is now **Bubbles**.

## Install or upgrade

1. Quit Stillwater if it is running (menu bar icon → **Quit**).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Stillwater** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy.
3. Open Stillwater. Your settings and population are kept.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust this release, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. The [installation guide](../INSTALL.md) has step-by-step instructions.

## Performance
Frame rates are unchanged from 1.2.0. With a full tank (120 residents) the extra bubbles and sand use about 4–5% more of one CPU core. Still mode, sleeping displays, and hidden windows still pause the animation completely.

## Known limitations
- A crab can occasionally appear to straddle a loach for a moment when they cross on the sand.
- Seen closely, the sand heaped against an air stone can look slightly flat along its lower edge.
- Motion speeds follow the **Swimming speed** setting, which scales every movement, turns included.

## Under the hood
- New `Bubbles.swift` and `Sand.swift`; the fish shader gained a bent fin-sheet scan, flaring fins, and a shrimp abdomen curl.
- 35 model tests now cover two-stage and hover turns, turn speeds, bed spacing, crab-on-loach overlap, sand, bubble and pellet physics, and shrimp and crab gaits; rendering checks run in CI.
- Research sources are listed in the [development notes](../DEVELOPMENT.md). Full history: [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
