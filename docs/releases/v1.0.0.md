# Stillwater 1.0.0

The first public release: a native freshwater aquarium wallpaper for Apple Silicon Macs, with three scenes, four fish species, gentle motion, and automatic day/night lighting.

## In this release

- Riverlight, Sunken Grove, and Willow Springs with crystal-clear water and plants.
- Harlequin rasboras, cherry barbs, pearl gouramis, and kuhli loaches; 0–30 of each.
- Live and Still modes, display-resolution PNG export, and permanent still wallpaper export.
- Neutral light/dark settings and Follow Mac / Day / Night lighting controls.
- Fish that notice food, approach, nibble, and drift apart, with a global **Control–Option–Command–F** shortcut.
- Pellets released in staggered sprinkles from changing locations, with varied size, rotation, drift, and sinking speed.
- Optional bubbles that respawn from different locations with varied timing, speed, and size.
- Configurable motion, brightness, particles, bubbles, and 15/30/60 fps targets.
- Local preferences, all assets bundled, no account or runtime network calls.
- Public source and project artwork under MIT.

## Downloads

- `Stillwater-1.0.0-macOS-arm64.dmg` — drag the app to Applications.
- `Stillwater-1.0.0-macOS-arm64.zip` — extract and move the app to Applications.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` — checksums for both archives.

**Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 14+.** The app is ad-hoc signed, **not Apple-notarized**. macOS may require **Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after the first launch attempt. Read the [installation guide](https://github.com/xindomusic/stillwater/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).

## Validation and limits

Deterministic model checks and native rendering/control checks passed locally. Tests cover feeding responses, pellet timing and bounds, bubble respawning, complete Still freezing, saved preferences, day/night changes, and display-resolution export. Details are in [VALIDATION.md](https://github.com/xindomusic/stillwater/blob/main/VALIDATION.md).

Rendering was tested on an M4 with macOS 27. Fish are layered 2D images with procedural motion. Richer general schooling, more physical desktop/display coverage, and sustained energy measurements remain future work.
