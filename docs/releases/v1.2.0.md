# Stillwater 1.2.0

Livelier, more natural residents, and solid fish from every angle.

- **Solid turns:** a fish seen head-on during a turn no longer shows water through its middle, and sharply bent turns no longer draw detached ghost copies.
- **Natural turning:** fish glide through banked U-turns instead of spinning in place, swinging nearer or farther as they turn. Large, slow fish trace a visible arc; small fish turn briskly. Fish climb and dive along a slope, and tip up when they strike at food.
- **Each fish its own dice:** every resident rolls its own seeded dice, with lifelike chance: irregular pauses between decisions, mostly small course changes with an occasional reversal, gently meandering paths, and a lasting temperament, so some fish are bolder, more restless, or more sociable than others.
- **Crabs and shrimp:** crabs scuttle sideways in quick stop-and-go bursts and rest their legs when they stop. Shrimp pick their way across the sand in small steps and dart about two body lengths away from fish that would eat them, tail first when the threat is ahead.
- **Tidier wallpaper stills:** older saved stills are cleaned up, keeping the newest eight, any still a display uses, and anything from the last two weeks.
- **Efficiency:** crowded feedings simulate in a fraction of a millisecond, floating particles draw in one GPU batch, and per-frame shader values are reused.

Your settings and population are preserved.

Model tests now run as named cases covering dice quality, turning, feeding, individuality, and crab and shrimp gaits. A new rendering check measures see-through pixels in head-on fish. See the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) and [development notes](../DEVELOPMENT.md).

Requires **Apple Silicon and macOS 14+**. The binary is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**; first-launch instructions are in the [installation guide](../INSTALL.md).
