# Stillwater 1.0.2

More depth in the water and smoother fish turns.

- **Near and far bubbles:** perspective size, distance contrast, and rise speed. Bubbles can pass behind or in front of fish.
- **Solid food pellets:** small rounded volumes with shaded faces, visible thickness, surface grain, and tumble. Each portion spans different distances, and pellets settle as they reach the sand.
- **Continuous turns:** the same photographed fish rotates on a curved body with thin fins, without switching between different pose images. Fin coverage softens as fish turn directly toward or away from you.
- Feeding reactions, day/night lighting, existing settings, and Still-mode freezing are preserved.

Quit the previous app before installing the DMG or ZIP. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14+. The app is ad-hoc signed, **not Apple-notarized**. [Installation instructions](https://github.com/xindomusic/stillwater/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).

Model and native rendering tests cover particle depth, overlap with fish, frozen tumble, and turn continuity. See [validation](https://github.com/xindomusic/stillwater/blob/main/VALIDATION.md) for details and limits.
