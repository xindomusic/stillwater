# Stillwater 1.0.3

More natural swimming with a smaller memory footprint.

- **Individual rhythms:** tail strokes vary in strength and cadence, with short glides and independently moving balancing fins. Faster swimming and acceleration recruit stronger strokes.
- **Articulated turns:** the head leads, the body curves, and the tail follows. Turning speed eases in and out instead of beginning at a fixed angular speed.
- **Less repeated work:** compact fish textures, shared scene textures, reused shader values, and fewer per-frame allocations. Hidden particles skip positioning work.
- Feeding, particle depth, day/night settings, and complete Still-mode freezing are preserved.

The repeated local comparison used about **30% less memory** in the default scene, with higher average frame rates. CPU results varied by mode. Measurements and their limits are documented in [validation](https://github.com/xindomusic/stillwater/blob/main/VALIDATION.md). This remains a lightweight photographic renderer, not a complete anatomical simulation.

Quit the previous app before installing the DMG or ZIP. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14+. The app is ad-hoc signed, **not Apple-notarized**. [Installation instructions](https://github.com/xindomusic/stillwater/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).
